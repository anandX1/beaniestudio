# DISBOARD-GUIDE.md — Free Discord growth, step by step

> Disboard.org is the biggest public Discord server directory. Research case:
> "1 member → 300 in 2 weeks" from listing + bumping alone. Cost: $0. Time:
> ~20 min setup + a 10-second command every 2 hours (rotating duty).

## One-time setup (~20 min)

1. **Go to disboard.org → "Add Server"** → sign in with Discord (OAuth, safe).
2. **Fill the listing like a landing page** (this is what converts):
   - **Server name:** `Beanie Studio — STATIC (5v1 Horror on Roblox)`
   - **Short description** (shows in list): `Free 5v1 horror on Roblox. Five salvagers, one blind Hunter who hunts by SOUND. No radar. Playtest nights, devlogs, launch crew.`
   - **Long description:** paste the game pitch, add 2–3 GIFs/clips, the playtest schedule, and the site link `https://beaniestudio.site`.
   - **Tags** (these are your search keywords — max is 5, choose like SEO): `roblox`, `horror`, `gaming`, `roblox-horror`, `indie`
3. **Invite the Disboard bot** to your server (it prompts you after listing) — give it a `#bump-log` channel.
4. In Discord, run **`/bump`** once in any channel the bot can see. Your listing is now live and ranked.
5. **Set the server icon + banner** to the same key art as the site (brand recognition when people hop between site ↔ Discord).

## The bump rhythm (the unsung hero part)

- `/bump` can be run **every 2 hours** — each bump pushes your server back to the top of the tag pages ("recently bumped" sorts first). This is the entire game.
- **Roblox players are global — the trap is bumping only in your own timezone.** Cover at least: EU evening (17–20 UTC), US evening (00–03 UTC), and one Asian-hours bump if anyone's awake.
- **Rotate the duty:** make a `#bump-reminders` channel, add the Disboard bot's auto-reminder (it pings a role when 2h pass), and give 2–3 trusted members permission to run `/bump`. You should never be the bottleneck.
- **Never pay for a bump service.** Free tier is the whole value; paid "boosts" are snake oil.

## What actually makes listing visitors JOIN (then stay)

Disboard visitors peek at the server before joining — Discord shows member count + online count. So:

1. **Online > 10 matters.** Schedule playtest nights and VC hangs — "recently active" servers convert far better.
2. **The welcome screen is your landing page:** one emoji-reaction role picker (📡 = launch notifications, 🎮 = playtest crew, 🎬 = content/creator role), pinned 30-second pitch with the best clip, clear rules.
3. **Rename your invite to track it:** Disboard lets people join via the listing — use a distinctive vanity invite so you can see the joins it drives (server settings → vanity URL if enabled, or a dedicated invite named "disboard" and check *Server Insights → invites* weekly).

## Realistic expectations

A polished listing with consistent bumps typically nets **5–20 joins/day** for a niche server. Over the 10-day sprint that's 50–200 members — the single most reliable free channel you have, and it keeps working after launch forever.
