## 0. The honest math (memorize this)

```
1,000,000 clip views  →  ~30–50k site/profile visits  →  ~8–12k captures
```

- **1M views is the viral case**, not the base case. Expected case at solid cadence:
  150–400k views → 5–15k visits → **1.5–3k captures**.
- A single breakout clip changes everything (one 500k-view clip ≈ 3 weeks of grinding).
- **The strategy is therefore: maximum shots on goal.** 3 clips/day × 10 days = 30
  entries into the lottery, each built on a hook with proven psychology (see pack).
- 10k happens only if at least one clip breaks out AND the capture machinery
  (already live) converts the spike. Both are now optimized.

**Kill criteria (be honest at day 5):** if day-5 cumulative captures < 400 AND best
clip < 5k views → the hooks aren't landing → switch Tier 1 hooks to Tier 3
(comment-bait formats) and double DM output. Do not keep grinding a dead approach.

## 2. Day-by-day schedule

**Daily constants (every day, ~2.5–3h):** 3 clips posted (TikTok, then YT Shorts +2h,
Reels +4h) · 10 creator DMs · Disboard bump (every 2h while awake — rotate with 2
trusted members) · reply to every comment within 2h · 15 min in own Discord VC.

- **Day 1 — Groundwork (heavier, ~4h):** the 9 listings (Disboard first, bot in,
  /bump working). Film 6 clips in ONE sitting (batch = week's material). Post clip #1.
  Set up #bump-reminders channel. Pin playtest page in Discord announcements.
- **Day 2 — First story post:** r/robloxgamedev post (story from the pack, link in
  comments only) at 14–19 UTC. Clips 2–4. First 10 DMs.
- **Day 3 — Playtest night #1:** announce 24h ahead via Discord + a clip about it.
  Run 3–5 full rounds with members. **Record everything** — this IS day 4–10's clip
  material. Post-playtest clip: "we playtested with the community, here's what broke."
- **Day 4 — aftermath content:** clip montage from playtest night + "your name in
  the credits" post in Discord. Announce playtest #2 (bigger slot).
- **Days 5–6 — volume + iterate:** check analytics: which hook tier is performing?
  Double down on that tier. Kill criteria check at day 5. Keep DMs at 10/day.
- **Day 7 — playtest night #2:** bigger, announced 48h ahead. Encourage members to
  bring ONE friend each (5v1 needs exactly that). This is the exponential mechanic.
- **Day 8 — creator wave 2:** follow up with every DM that replied (warm leads now).
  Offer exclusive: "first public playtest build" the day before launch.
- **Day 9 — press + final listings:** DevForum post with real numbers ("76 → X
  members in 9 days, here's our full playbook" — devs LOVE these and they travel).
- **Day 10 — The announcement:** "1 week to doors" post everywhere + final clip.
  Every captured human gets the Roblox-follow reminder: "following the community =
  launch-day notification in your Roblox app."

## 3. Channel playbooks (what research says actually works)

1. **Short-form clips = the engine.** Post the SAME clip to TikTok, YT Shorts, IG
   Reels staggered 2h apart (algorithm-suppression avoids crossposting detection:
   slight caption variation helps). Hook in first line of on-screen text; first 2
   seconds decide everything; 25–40s ideal length; end with a question (comments
   boost distribution).
2. **Disboard bump chain = the unsung hero.** Reliable 5–20 joins/day for a polished
   listing. Costs nothing but a 2h reminder. Most devs never do it consistently.
3. **Experience Notifications = the on-platform multiplier.** Every Roblox community
   follow = a push notification the day you launch. This is why follows are weighted
   equally with Discord joins in the 10k count.
4. **Reddit = trust bombs, not link dumps.** One great story post ("we removed the
   radar and playtests got 10x tenser") outperforms 50 link drops and can hit
   r/robloxgamedev's front page (30k+ devs see it).
5. **Creator DMs = the launch-week force multiplier.** 70 DMs over 10 days at a 10%
   yes-rate = ~7 micro-streamers with real audiences. Horror = reaction content;
   they NEED new games. Give them a private slot + full freedom.
6. **Playtest nights = retention + content factory.** They convert members into
   evangelists AND generate the next week's clips for free.

## 4. Overhyped stuff you should NOT do (research-verified)

- **Paid ads before launch** — CPMs eat indie budgets for vanity impressions.
- **X/Instagram posting as primary channel** — no free API, tiny Roblox-audience
  overlap. Post there (2 min from the pack), never *count* on it.
- **Mass-DMing servers / buying members** — bans, dead weight, kills Discovery.
- **Press releases** — no gaming press covers pre-launch Roblox indies; save the
  story for a real milestone (10k members IS that milestone — then pitch it).
- **"Post everywhere once"** — distribution requires cadence. 1 post per platform
  per week = invisible.

## 6. The 10,000 reality check

- **Base case (~70% probability): 1.5–3k waiting.** A genuinely strong pre-launch
  base for an indie Roblox horror game — plus 30 clips of content, a warmer Discord,
  creator relationships, and a site that indexes instantly.
- **Good case (~25%): 4–7k.** One semi-viral clip (100–300k) + solid conversion.
- **Viral case (~5%): 10k+ ✅.** Needs a breakout clip (500k+) — the 30-clip volume
  strategy exists precisely to maximize the odds of this single event.
- **The compounding asset regardless of outcome:** every clip, DM, listing, and
  devlog keeps working after day 10 — and 3k + Experience Notifications on launch
  morning can outperform 10k cold members anyway.

The sprint in one sentence: **ship 30 sharp hooks through the fastest channel, let
the already-built capture machinery convert, keep every promise, and check the
math at day 5 without ego.**
