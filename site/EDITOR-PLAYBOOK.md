# EDITOR-PLAYBOOK.md — turning your editor into a player magnet

> Your editor is the single highest-leverage human in this sprint. The engine
> writes the hooks and captions (`npm run engine:all` → `content/`), the site
> captures whoever clicks — the editor's job is the 30 seconds in between.

## The division of labor

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| Content engine (your PC) | Harvests real search keywords, drafts hooks + captions + hashtags into `content/drafts-<date>.md`, generates the editor brief |
| You (5 min/day) | Tick `[x]` on good drafts, tweak the rest — the anti-flag human gate |
| **Editor** | Cuts 3 clips/day (25–40s), puts the hook as on-screen text in seconds 0–2, exports per specs below, uploads to TikTok/Shorts/Reels (staggered 2h), pastes the approved captions |
| Site + CI | Indexes everything, captures visitors (Discord/Roblox follow), reports which UTM/source converted |

## Non-negotiable clip rules (these decide virality, research-verified)

1. **Second 0–2 is everything.** Hook text on screen + the game's most alarming
   audio frame first. No logos, no intros, no "hey guys". The algorithm decides
   distribution almost entirely on first-2s watch-through.
2. **25–40 seconds.** Long enough for tension, short enough for rewatch loops.
3. **Native vertical 9:16** for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. 1080×1920, loud audio (horror
   IS audio — never let a platform crush the footsteps).
4. **End on a question or an unresolved beat** ("would YOU have opened it?") —
   comments are a distribution multiplier.
5. **One clip = one idea.** A sprint-death. A locker save. Never a highlights mashup.

## The CTA chain (how a viewer becomes a player)

- **TikTok:** link-in-bio → `beaniestudio.site/playtest/` · pinned comment: the pitch + Discord link
- **Shorts/Reels:** pinned comment same · description from the drafts file
- **Every caption** already carries the Discord + site (engine includes them, UTM-tagged)
- In-video text CTA at the end, max 3 words: **"Discord in bio"** — that's it.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon:** you hand the editor the week's briefs (`content/` folder + MARKETING-PACK.md hooks)
- **Daily:** 3 clips out (editor), captions approved by you (engine)
- **Playtest nights (day 3 & 7):** editor gets raw footage same night; next-day clip =
  "community playtest #1 — here's what broke" (these convert REALLY well: real humans,
  real screams, social proof)
- **Fri:** 15-min review — which hook tier won? Engine drafts next week biased toward it

## The feedback loop (make the editor's job winnable)

Editor needs to SEE results or they're cutting blind:
- Share TikTok/Shorts analytics weekly (views, avg watch %, follows per clip)
- Watch-% is the metric: >70% average = format working; <40% = hooks or pacing fail
- Any clip >25k views = immediately cut 2 variants of it (the algorithm is telling
  you what it wants; feed it)

## What to pay your editor in (if not cash)

- **Creator role in Discord + name in the launch credits**
- **First access to playtest builds** (they'll want to cut their own gameplay)
- **The growth is their portfolio:** "I grew a Roblox horror game from 76 to X"
  is a portfolio line worth real money to them. Show them the counter going up.
