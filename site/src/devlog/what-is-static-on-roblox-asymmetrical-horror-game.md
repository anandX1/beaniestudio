## The core loop of our 5v1 horror asymmetrical game on Roblox  

In **STATIC**, five Scrappers race to grab scrap from a wrecked shuttle. One Hunter stalks the corridors. Scrappers need speed, but every dropped scrap or panicked mini‑game on a generator gives the Hunter a clue. The Hunter uses on‑screen highlights, sprints to the loudest source, and relies on proximity voice chat to track terrified teammates.

## Why we chose an asymmetrical horror approach  

We wanted tension that feels personal and unpredictable—humans on both sides. So we built an asymmetrical experience where the power balance shifts each match. Scrappers can hide, throw decoys, and set noise traps. Hunters can only hear. That forces both sides to think in sound, not sight, which is rare on Roblox.

## Building the sound‑first mechanics with an asymmetrical horror game kit  

Our toolkit lets us attach audio emitters to every interactive object. When a Scrapper picks up a fuel can, a low hum starts; drop it, and the hum spikes and echoes. The Hunter’s HUD is just a waveform that reacts to volume, keeping the focus on listening instead of looking.

## Understanding the asymmetrical horror game meaning for players  

The term describes games where players have different abilities and goals, creating natural tension. In **STATIC**, the Hunter acts fast, while Scrappers have full vision but limited speed and time. That contrast drives fear and pushes Scrappers to work together.

## Optimizing for asymmetrical horror game mobile experiences  

We stripped graphics to run smoothly on low‑end phones. We target 30 fps even on a 2015 iPhone 5. Audio processing runs on a lightweight engine, so sound cues stay crisp on modest hardware.

## Brainstorming asymmetrical horror game name ideas  

Early sprints saw titles like “Echo Hunt” and “Silence Rift.” “STATIC” stuck because it evokes the wreck’s electrical remnants and the tense silence of each match.

## Bringing STATIC to the Roblox platform  

Roblox gives us cross‑play, letting PC and mobile users join the same lobby without extra setup. Our community hub at https://beaniestudio.site and the Discord server keep players up to date on updates and playtest nights.
