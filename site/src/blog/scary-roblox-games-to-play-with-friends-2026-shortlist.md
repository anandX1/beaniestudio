Every horror game is better with friends, but Roblox makes it free, instant, and crossplay between a phone, a laptop and a console in the same lobby. We build STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter, a 5v1 horror game for Roblox, so squads are literally our business. This list is the shortlist we give people who ask what to play tonight - five games we have actually played in groups, what each one is good at, and who it fits.

A quick note on honesty: some of these games are rivals of ours. They still belong on the list. A recommendation you can trust is worth more than a list that only praises the thing you made.

## What makes a horror game work with a group

Solo horror is about tension. Group horror is about *talking*. The best scary Roblox games for friends share three traits:

- **Everyone has a job.** If two players can just stand in a corner while the others do everything, the bored players leave. Good group horror gives each person something the team needs.
- **Voice chat changes everything.** Roblox's proximity voice chat turns a scary moment into a shared one. Hearing a friend's actual voice shake is ten times better than a text message that says "behind you."
- **Rounds are short.** Fifteen-minute rounds mean a squad can fit in three runs before dinner. Games that take an hour to get to the good part do not survive group play.

Keep those three in mind as you read. They are exactly the criteria we used to build our own game, too.

## The shortlist

**1. STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter (that's us).** Five Scrappers salvage scrap from a wrecked facility while the sixth player, the Hunter, stalks the halls. No radar, no minimap, no wallhacks for anyone - you track the danger by sound and by instinct. First hit knocks a Scrapper down; the Hunter can pick you up and carry you to a siphon pod, and your squad has seconds to save you. Rounds are short, voice chat matters constantly, and it runs on low-end phones. It is free, and the playtest community is open - the Discord link is at the bottom.

**2. A classic hide-and-seek horror round.** Search Roblox for hide and seek horror games and you will find several long-running ones. The formula is simple and it works: one monster, everyone hides, tension builds as the monster gets closer. Perfect for 4-6 players, great on mobile, and the rounds are quick. Downside: limited depth. Great as a warm-up game, less so as the main event of an evening.

**3. The big lobby horror games.** Several popular titles run 8-12 players with one or two killers. They are loud, chaotic and genuinely funny with the right squad. The trade-off is that with that many players, you are rarely the star - you spend a lot of time watching. We think the sweet spot is 5-6 players, where everyone is busy and everyone matters. That number is not an accident: it is why STATIC is 5 Scrappers and 1 Hunter.

**4. Story-driven horror experiences.** A cluster of Roblox games plays more like interactive horror movies - scripted scares, chapters, less replayability. With a friend who has not seen the story yet, they are fantastic. Once you both know the twists, the replay value drops hard. Treat them like renting a film, not buying one.

**5. Mic-based horror games.** A growing genre where the game literally hears you - monsters react to your microphone. Terrifying the first time, hilarious the tenth. A word of warning for parents: these need voice chat permissions and sometimes age verification on Roblox, so check the settings before younger players join.

## Picking the right game for your squad size

- **2 players:** story horror or duo-mode hide and seek. In STATIC you can queue as a duo, but a 1v1 Hunter round is the spicy version - one of you hunts, one of you hides, nobody is neutral.
- **3-4 players:** most games hit their stride here. Enough bodies for chaos, few enough that everyone stays busy.
- **5-6 players:** this is the sweet spot for STATIC's design. Five Scrappers have to split up to cover the deposit areas, which means the Hunter is never sure how many people are between them and the next room.
- **7+ players:** the big lobby games. Fun, chaotic, and a good time even for the players who mostly watch.

## Three tips that make group horror night better

1. **Use proximity voice, not just party chat.** Half the fun of scary Roblox games with friends is hearing panic in real time. Party voice is convenient; in-game proximity voice is *theater*.
2. **Agree on a no-rage rule before round one.** Horror games produce betrayals - someone closes a door, someone runs, someone gets someone else caught. Decide in advance that it is funny, not a fight.
3. **Let the newest player survive or die first.** Their reaction is the content. If you are playing with someone new, protect their first-round experience - it decides whether they come back.
