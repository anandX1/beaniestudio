## What Is an Asymmetrical Horror Game?

Asymmetrical horror games are all about balance and imbalance. In this genre, players don’t share the same team size, abilities, or goals. Instead, one player usually faces a larger group with a totally different role. The lone player becomes the threat; the others must survive.

That mismatch builds tension. The hunter relies on raw power or special senses, while the survivors need stealth, good communication, and speed. Every round feels like high‑stakes hide‑and‑seek, and a single mistake can end the game instantly.

## Inside Our 5v1 Roblox Title

We’re putting those ideas into our upcoming 5v1 horror game on Roblox, launching Q4 2026. One player takes the Hunter role—a blind creature that tracks you by sound and footstep vibrations. The five Scrappers have no weapons. Their job is to move through industrial ruins, grab fuel cells, and power the extraction gates before the Hunter catches them.

Running makes noise, alerting the Hunter right away. Walking keeps you quiet, but the shrinking perimeter makes you vulnerable. Scrappers constantly juggle speed against safety.

## Optimized Performance and Cross‑Play

We built the game from scratch to work on PC and low‑end mobile. Frame drops kill horror; timing matters. We spent months profiling mobile memory, cutting draw calls, and fine‑tuning spatial audio so everyone hears threats the same way.
