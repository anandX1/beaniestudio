## What Is an Asymmetrical Horror Game?

Most multiplayer games are symmetrical: both teams get the same guns, the same maps, the same rules. Asymmetrical horror breaks that on purpose. One side gets power. The other side gets numbers. Neither side plays the same game, and that mismatch is where the fear comes from.

If you have ever played hide-and-seek as an adult, you already understand the genre. The seeker is stronger, but the hiders know something the seeker does not: where they are. The moment that information leaks — a laugh, a snapped twig — the whole game changes.

That is the asymmetrical horror game meaning in one sentence: **two different games stacked on top of each other, where the tension comes from what one side knows and the other does not.**

Dead by Daylight built the genre on PC and console. Left 4 Dead built its co-op cousin. But asymmetrical horror on mobile — on phones, with touch controls, with friends you already talk to — is still wide open. That is the gap we are building STATIC to fill.

## Inside Our 5v1 Roblox Title

**STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter** is a 5v1 horror game on Roblox, launching Q4 2026, free, with crossplay between mobile, PC, and console. One player is the Hunter. Five players are Scrappers. Here is how each side actually plays.

### The Scrappers' game: work fast, stay quiet

Five Scrappers drop into a wrecked industrial facility full of salvage. Their job sounds boring on paper: collect scrap and feed it to **deposit areas** scattered across the map. There are five of them, each with its own quota, and every quota you complete triggers a calibration minigame — timing taps under pressure while somewhere out there, the Hunter gets closer.

The catch is weight. You can carry a lot of small scrap, but heavy items cap you at 10% overload — enough to still move, not enough to ever feel safe. Full pockets make you slow, loud, and tempting.

### The Hunter's game: punish every mistake

Here is the design rule that shapes the whole game: **the Hunter gets nothing for free.** No radar. No minimap. No wallhacks — for anyone. The Hunter only gains information when *we* make noise, and we only make noise when something goes wrong.

A calibration tap missed? That rings out. A teammate thrown into a siphon pod? The alarm carries. A heavy scrap drag across the floor? You will hear exactly where it is going. The Hunter is a predator of mistakes, which means Scrappers who play clean are nearly invisible — and Scrappers who panic feed the predator.

When the Hunter does catch you, it is not a one-button kill. The first swing knocks you down and leaves you crawling. From there it gets personal: the Hunter can haul you over a shoulder — collar drag — and pitch you into a bio-siphon pod to lock you away. Your teammates see the pod light up. They can come break you out. Or you can gamble on a 5% self-escape that either frees you or finishes you.

### Winning both games

When every deposit area hits its quota, the facility's two exit doors power up — and only one of them opens, chosen at random. The final sprint to the correct door, with a Hunter who now knows exactly where everyone is heading, is the loudest quiet moment in the game.

## Why 5v1 Works on Mobile

Horror games usually assume you are alone in a dark room with headphones. Most Roblox players are on phones, in bright rooms, with the volume low. So STATIC does not ask you to hear a pin drop. It asks you to keep the facility from making noise in the first place — a skill that works whether you are on a flagship PC or a three-year-old phone.

We target smooth performance on low-end devices, and every input — including mashing to escape a carry — maps to one big button on touch. Crossplay means your five friends can join from whatever they own.
