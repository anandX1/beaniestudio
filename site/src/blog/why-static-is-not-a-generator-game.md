Before we started STATIC, we played a lot of asymmetrical horror on Roblox, and we kept noticing the same thing. New games competed on graphics and realism, but the survivors' job barely changed: find the machines, fix them, open the gate, leave. The killer was the interesting part. Surviving was a checklist.

STATIC started from one question: what if the survivors' side was a game of its own?

## Scrap instead of generators

Instead of repairing fixed machines, Scrappers in STATIC have to **find and move weight**. There are 14 kinds of scrap spread around the bunker, from a 3 kg access card to a 140 kg nuclear reactor core, and three crushers that need 400 kg between them.

That one change creates decisions a generator never asks you to make:

- **What's worth carrying?** Small scrap fits in two backpack slots and costs you nothing. Heavy scrap slows you down and turns off your flashlight.
- **Where do we go?** Crushers spawn in different places every round, so the route is never the same twice.
- **Do we split up?** Two people on a console finish it much faster. Someone still has to haul scrap to the next crusher.

## Minigames instead of one skill check

Every full crusher unlocks a console of seven quick minigames, drawn from a pool of thirteen: wires, pipes, sonar, lasers, a Geiger counter and more. Failing one doesn't cost progress, but it makes noise, and noise is how you get found. We wanted the tasks to feel like *work under pressure*, not a timing button.

## Noise, without making the whole game about it

Early versions of STATIC leaned almost entirely on sound: the Hunter tracked players by the noise they made. It sounded great on paper. In practice, a lot of Roblox plays on phone speakers, and "be quiet" turned into "stand still", which isn't fun for anyone.

So noise stayed, but in specific places where it's a choice. Your bell always summons the Worm. A failed console faults loudly. Staying in a locker too long gives you away. And Jingles got Bloodlust, a reveal every 50 to 80 seconds, so hiding forever stops being a strategy.

## Where it goes next

The beta is one map and one Hunter. Next on the list are new modes (including ones where the Hunter role changes hands mid-round), a second map, a second Hunter and events. If you've played and have opinions, the [Discord](https://discord.gg/z8kPT6cRbG) is where they turn into updates.
