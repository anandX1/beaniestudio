STATIC's first public beta opens on Roblox on **10 October 2026**. It's free, it runs on PC and mobile, and it's the first version of the game we're happy for anyone to play. Here is exactly what's in it, with no padding.

## The short version

STATIC is a co-op horror game. Up to six players (the Scrappers) search an abandoned bunker for scrap and haul it to three crushers. One more player is the Hunter: **Jingles**, a clockwork jester. Fill the crushers, clear their consoles, and get out through the Intake Chute before Jingles gets you.

## What's in the beta

- **One map.** An underground bunker with rooms including the Nuclear Reactor, Security, Control Room, Medbay, Interrogation Room, Storage Bay, Cargo Dock, Airlock and Central Room.
- **One Hunter.** Jingles moves at 13.5 studs per second, against a Scrapper sprint of 13. It has a Dash, a two-hit attack with a red warning zone, and Bloodlust, which reveals every Scrapper for a moment every 50 to 80 seconds. [Full Jingles breakdown](/guide/jingles/).
- **14 scrap items.** From a 3 kg security access card to a 140 kg nuclear reactor core, each with a chance to roll Rare (×1.5 weight) or Charged (×3). [The full scrap list](/guide/scrap/).
- **3 crushers.** Quotas of 120, 130 and 150 kg, in different places every round.
- **13 console minigames.** Every crusher's console picks 7 of them. Two players can work one console at once. [How crushers work](/guide/crushers/).
- **Pods and rescues.** A 90-second pod timer, 3-second rescues, and an overload system that punishes a Hunter for camping. [Pods explained](/guide/capture-and-rescue/).
- **The Worm.** It lives under the floor, answers your bell, and backs off from a flashlight. [Worm guide](/guide/worm/).
- **Voice chat** for players with Roblox voice enabled.

## What isn't in it yet

We'd rather tell you now than have you find out: there are no codes yet, no cosmetics shop, no second map and no second Hunter. All of those are planned. New modes are planned too, including ones where the Hunter role passes between players during a round. The Discord votes on what comes first.

## It's a beta

Things will break. Animations on Jingles' custom rig are still the part we fight with most. If you hit a bug, report it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/z8kPT6cRbG), where it goes straight to the three of us.

See you in the bunker. Leave the bell alone.
