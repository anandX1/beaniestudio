## The 30-second version

**STATIC** is a 5-versus-1 horror game on Roblox. Five players — Scrappers — loot a wrecked industrial facility for scrap and feed it to deposit areas to power the exits. One player — the Hunter — stalks them, catches them, and locks them away in siphon pods. Scrappers win by escaping. The Hunter wins by containing everyone. No radar, no minimap, no wallhacks for anyone. Free to play, crossplay between mobile, PC, and console, launching Q4 2026.

If that is all you needed: the game's [play page](/play/) has the Roblox link, and our [Discord](https://discord.gg/z8kPT6cRbG) runs open playtests. If you want to understand *why* it plays the way it does, keep reading.

## A single match, start to finish

Every match of this 5v1 horror game has the same heartbeat, but no two rounds feel alike. Here is what actually happens in a round.

**1. Loot.** The five Scrappers spread out through the wreck. Scrap comes in small pieces you can pocket freely and heavy pieces that cap how overloaded you can get at 10% — heavy enough to slow you, never enough to be a death sentence. Every piece you carry is a piece the Hunter can hear drop.

**2. Deposit.** Scrap gets fed to five deposit areas, each with its own quota. Filling one triggers a calibration minigame: timed taps, and every miss rings out across the facility. This is the core gamble of STATIC — the only way to win is also the only way to tell the Hunter where you are.

**3. Survive.** Between deposits, the Hunter hunts. It cannot see you. It gains information only when the facility makes noise — a failed calibration, a pod alarm, a heavy drag. When it catches a Scrapper, the first swing knocks them down, crawling. From there the Hunter can pick them up, carry them over a shoulder, and pitch them into a bio-siphon pod. Locked teammates get highlighted for the whole team: someone can come rescue them, or the trapped player can gamble on a 5% self-escape that either frees them or finishes them.

**4. Escape.** When all five deposit areas hit quota, two exit doors power up — and the facility randomly opens exactly one. Five exhausted Scrappers sprint for a door the Hunter now knows they need. That final minute is why people stream this game.

## What the Hunter can and cannot do

Asymmetry only works if both sides feel powerful. The Hunter's power: it is fast, it is patient, and every swing slows it for a moment — so its rhythm matters more than its speed. Its weakness: it starts every match blind and stays that way until *someone* makes a mistake. A Scrapper who plays clean is a ghost. A Scrapper who panics is dinner.

That single rule — the Hunter punishes mistakes, it does not cheat — is what makes people call STATIC scary on Roblox. In most Roblox horror games, the monster sees you and you run. Here, the monster is *listening for the facility to betray you*, and you are the one operating the facility.

## Why we built it on Roblox

Roblox gives us three things no other platform bundles together:

- **Crossplay by default.** Your friend on a phone, your friend on a PC, your cousin on a console — one lobby, no setup.
- **Zero install.** The scariest moment in most horror games is a 60-gigabyte download. STATIC loads in the app people already have.
- **A social graph that spreads.** When someone plays, their friends see it. Horror is a spectator sport, and Roblox makes spectating one tap away.

The trade-off is hardware: we optimize for phones first, which shapes every design decision — big touch targets, one-button interactions, and a horror engine built on *behavior* instead of jump-scare graphics.

## What is next for STATIC

The launch roadmap has two big additions already in design: **classes** — Scrapper roles that resist stuns, slow the Hunter, or carry more scrap — and a creator program that lets streamers and their chats interact with live matches. Both are covered in depth on our [creators page](/creators/) and in the [blog](/blog/), and playtest feedback drives what ships first.

Want to be part of that? The [Discord](https://discord.gg/z8kPT6cRbG) is where playtests get announced, and every balance number in the game has been argued over by the people playing it.
