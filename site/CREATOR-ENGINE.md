## 2. The creator loop — his two "really small features" that drove massive traction

> His words: *"one of the reasons the game got so much traction is actually two really
> small features."*

**Feature A — gift → keypress → in-game effect.** When a TikTok viewer sends a gift,
Tikfinity simulates a keyboard press, and the game reacts (a key gets pressed, an effect
fires). Chat literally purchases interactions with the streamer. This makes the STREAM
a game between streamer and chat — and every gift-event is a highlight moment.

**Feature B — creator role + creator-only menu.** Verified streamers got a special
group role and an in-game menu just for them. Status + access = streamers identified
with the game and kept playing it on camera.

**Why this is even STRONGER for STATIC than for his game:** his game's chat interaction
was "press a key." STATIC's is **"the Hunter heard that."** Horror + chat control is
a content multiplier: chat triggers a fake footstep, the streamer's teammates panic,
the clip writes itself. Fear is the most clip-friendly emotion on short-form video.
