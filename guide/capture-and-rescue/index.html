Pods and rescue: what happens when you're caught

OFFICIAL GUIDE · CHECKED AGAINST THE BETA BUILD · UPDATED 2026-10-08

Getting caught in STATIC isn't the end of your round. Jingles has to knock you down, carry you to a pod, and then keep you there for 90 seconds while your team tries to get you out.

1. Getting downed

  • Jingles needs 2 hits to down you. The first leaves you at half health.
  • While downed you can crawl at 1.5 studs per second, or 6 if you push it.
  • A teammate can revive you by holding for 5 seconds.
  • Jingles can finish you on the spot in 2 seconds, or pick you up.

2. Being carried

Jingles drags you to a pod. While you're being carried, tap to fill the struggle meter. Fill it and you break free, and Jingles is stunned for 1.2 seconds. Carrying also slows Jingles to 10.8 studs per second and turns off its Dash.

3. The pod

  • The timer starts at 90 seconds.
  • If no teammate is left free to rescue you, it runs twice as fast.
  • Caught again later? The timer doesn't reset to 90. It picks up where it left off, but never below 30 seconds.
  • When it hits zero, you're out of the round and back in the lobby.

The 5% gamble

Once you're sealed in, you get exactly one self-escape attempt with a 5% chance to work. If it fails, 15 seconds come off your timer. Use it when nobody is coming.

4. Rescuing a teammate

  • Get within 7 studs of the pod and hold E for 3 seconds. No item needed.
  • The rescued player gets 5 seconds where they can't be hit, plus their speed back.
  • Rescues are fast. Time them for when Jingles is carrying someone else or has just used its Dash.

5. Anti-camping: overload

Jingles can't stand guard at a pod forever. While the Hunter lingers nearby, an overload meter fills, faster the closer it stands. When it maxes out, the pod knocks Jingles back and stuns it, frees the captive with a shield, and burns out for 20 seconds.
