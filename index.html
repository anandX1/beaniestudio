Mission File

STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter

PRE-LAUNCH
Read the full briefExpand

One player takes the Hunter — faster, stronger, built to dominate the map. Four to five Scrappers play the other side: outmatched, working together, relying on stealth over strength.

Sprint, drop something heavy, or yell over comms and the Hunter's radar lights up exactly where you are. No player can carry the heavy scrap alone — someone's always got to come help, right when the Hunter is closest.

5 v 1
Four to five Scrappers face one Hunter, every round.
5–10 min

Crossplay
