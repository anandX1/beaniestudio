Studio

Beanie Studio is a small team building for Roblox. Our first release is STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter — an asymmetrical 5-vs-1 horror game where a team of Scrappers salvage fuel from a wrecked shuttle while one Hunter stalks the wreck looking for them. Tense when you're hiding, chaotic when you're not — the ragdolls, shoves, and clumsy escapes are half the point.

Mission File

STATIC: Salvage vs Hunter

CLASSIFIED — PRE-LAUNCH

One player takes the Hunter — faster, stronger, built to dominate the map. Four to five Scrappers play the other side: outmatched, working together, and relying on stealth over strength. Sprint, drop something heavy, or yell over comms and the Hunter's radar lights up exactly where you are.

Rounds are short and built to replay. No player can carry the heavy scrap alone — someone's always got to come help, right when the Hunter is closest.

Match

5 Scrappers vs 1 Hunter

Round length

5–10 minutes
