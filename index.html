The map

Tour the bunker

One map in the beta, 8+ rooms. Every screenshot is from the game. Swipe to look around.

Full map guide →

  1. 01 / 08

    Central Room

    The hub where every round starts, built around a giant amber globe on a red-lit platform. Walkways lead off it toward the rest of the bunker.

  2. 02 / 08

    Cargo Dock

    The biggest open space in the bunker: a battered shuttle, a forklift, stacked crates and catwalks overhead, lit in red warning light.

  3. 03 / 08

    Storage Bay

    Long aisles of floor-to-ceiling shelving packed with crates. Plenty to search, and plenty of corners you can’t see past.

  4. 04 / 08

    Control Room

    Banks of consoles under a wall of blue screens and a glowing holographic world map. One of the brightest rooms in the bunker.

  5. 05 / 08

    Nuclear Reactor

    Behind an “Authorized personnel only” door: red-glowing coils, green-lit reactor tanks and very little light to see by.

  6. 06 / 08

    Medbay

    Rows of stretchers, blue privacy curtains and supply shelves. The long centre aisle leaves nowhere to hide when Jingles walks it.

  7. 07 / 08

    Security

    Desks, monitors and a server rack in cold blue light, reached through a corridor with a DANGER electrical cabinet.

  8. 08 / 08

    Interrogation Room

    Steel shelving and desks under harsh light, watched through a red-lit observation window.

It's not only Jingles

Two more ways to lose

⤢

Under the floor

The Worm

Ring your bell and it surfaces 3–5 seconds later. It grabs, drains 8–20% health, and leaves.

Hold your flashlight on it while it rears up and it backs off.

How to survive the Worm →
⤢

After the second hit

The Bio-Siphon Pod

Caught? 90 seconds. A teammate needs 3 to break you out. Alone, the clock runs twice as fast.

Camping a pod overloads it, stuns Jingles and sets you free.

Why we built it

Not another generator game

Survivors shouldn't just fix machines. In STATIC they explore, choose what to carry and split the work. Read why.

Aspect Most asymmetrical horror STATIC
Objective Repair a fixed set of machines Find, carry and crush 400 kg of scrap across the whole map
Tasks One repeated skill check 13 console minigames, 7 per crusher
Threats One killer Jingles, plus Worms that surface where it gets loud
Teamwork Mostly side by side Two players per console, 3-second pod rescues, one shared weight goal
The escape Open the gate A 60-second window and a personal UFO beam-out
