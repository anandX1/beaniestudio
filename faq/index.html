Intelligence file

Questions, answered

Everything we can publicly say right now. Anything not listed here is either undecided or being tested — the Discord hears it first.

When does STATIC release? +

We are targeting Q4 2026. The countdown on the home page tracks the start of the launch window, and the Discord gets every schedule update first.

How much will STATIC cost? +

Nothing. STATIC is free to play on Roblox at launch. If you have Roblox installed, you already have everything you need.

What platforms does STATIC support? +

Mobile, PC, and console — all in the same matches. Performance is tuned to run on iPhone-8-class phones, so the whole squad can play together regardless of device.

How many players are in a match? +

Four to five Scrappers versus one Hunter — up to six players per round, with rounds running five to ten minutes.

What is the game about? +

Scrappers salvage fuel from a wrecked shuttle while one player, the Hunter, stalks the wreck. Scrappers are outmatched and win by stealth, teamwork, and nerve. The Hunter is faster and stronger, and hunts entirely by sound.

How does the Hunter track players? +

By sound alone. There is no radar and no minimap — for anyone. Sprinting, dropping heavy objects, and yelling over comms all carry through the wreck. Move quietly or do not move at all.

Is STATIC scary or funny? +

Both, on purpose. The hiding is genuinely tense, but ragdoll physics, shoves, and clumsy escapes turn near-misses into clips. The best rounds are terrifying right up until they are hilarious.

How can I playtest or help? +

Join the Discord. Playtests, balance feedback, and bug reports all happen there, and regular contributors get credited in the facility.
